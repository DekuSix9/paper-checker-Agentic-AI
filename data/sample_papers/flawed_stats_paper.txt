Deep Learning Acceleration via Quantum-Inspired Heuristics

Abstract
We propose a novel quantum-inspired heuristic algorithm that speeds up neural network optimization by 10x over standard SGD.

1. Introduction
Modern deep learning models require immense computational resources. We present a fast heuristic that eliminates GPU bottlenecks.

2. Related Work
Many optimization algorithms exist, but ours is faster than all of them.

3. Method
We run our method on a small synthetic dataset of n = 12 samples. Hyperparameters were tuned manually until optimal accuracy was observed.

4. Results
Our algorithm achieved 99.9% accuracy on the test set. p = 0.045 was obtained. No baseline comparisons or standard deviation error bars are reported as the single-run test was conclusive.

5. Conclusion
Our quantum-inspired optimizer outperforms traditional deep learning optimizers.
